How I work

Payments are trust made visible. I love to make every state of the money understandable — especially when something goes wrong.

01

Design the failure path first

Timeouts, reversals and pending settlements are where trust is won or lost. I map recovery before polish.

02

Reveal complexity gradually

Progressive disclosure is a thing I have mastered. It helps me reveal to the user what steps are important without hiding what matters.

03

Strategy and craft, together

I lead with product thinking and still ship pixels — working side by side with Engineering, Product, CX and Marketing.
