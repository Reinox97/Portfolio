About

I design financial products that make moving money feel effortless.

I’m Ifeoluwa — a Product Design Lead at Payaza Africa (78 Financials), a Lagos-based fintech building cross-border payments and financial services.

I lead a design team of 10 people across the group and work hand in hand with the Engineering, Product, CX and Marketing teams. My days move between product strategy and the details: shaping roadmaps with PMs in the morning, sweating a settlement status screen in the afternoon.

I’ve got over 7 years in fintech. I’ve learned the infrastructure underneath the interface and know fintech a lot more deeply because of it. Knowing how money actually moves is what lets me design for how people feel about it.

Away from the screen, I’m drawn to good design in every form. I love gaming (FC Pro Player), playing the piano and swimming.
